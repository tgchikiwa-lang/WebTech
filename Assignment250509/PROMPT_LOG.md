# PROMPT_LOG

## 1. Exact prompt used

I am a second-year Computer Information Systems student creating a one-page semantic HTML5 profile/mini-portfolio for my Web Technologies course.
>Write a short About Me bio that introduces me as a student interested in technology and the connection between business and IT.
> Use simple, natural English. Keep it student-like, avoid exaggerated claims, avoid clichés, and do not invent achievements or experience.
>Write one short paragraph of about 80-100 words.

## 2. AI's raw output

l am a second-year Computer Information Systems student. I enjoy learning how technology can solve practical problems and help people and organisations work more effectively. 
My goal is to continue developing my technical and communication skills while gaining practical experience that will help me contribute to technology-focused projects and opportunities in the future.

## 3. Final edited version

 am a second-year Computer Information Systems student at Africa University. I enjoy learning how technology can solve practical problems and help people and organisations work more effectively. I am organised, determined and interested in building my skills in both information technology and business.

My goal is to continue developing my technical and communication skills while gaining practical experience that will help me contribute to technology-focused projects and opportunities in the future.

## 4. Reflection

In the final version, I changed the wording to sound more natural and personal. I added the name of my university to make the description more accurate. These edits helped the paragraph sound more like my own voice and made it easier to read and understand.
